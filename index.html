Professional Journey

The Story of Dr. Apkesha Patel

Dr. Apkesha Patel is a dedicated Ayurvedic physician (BAMS) whose medical path has been shaped by an unwavering commitment to authentic holistic healthcare and grassroots community service.

Service at Government Primary Health Centre (PHC)

For over four to five years, Dr. Apkesha served as an AYUSH Medical Officer within Gujarat's public health infrastructure. In rural and semi-urban Primary Health Centres, an AYUSH medical officer is on the frontline of community well-being, diagnosing a wide spectrum of health complaints ranging from seasonal epidemics and maternal nutrition to chronic metabolic and joint degenerations.

This extensive experience grounded her clinical approach in reality. She learned that sustainable recovery cannot come from one-size-fits-all prescriptions or complex regimens that patients cannot follow in their daily lives. Instead, authentic healing occurs when classical Ayurvedic principles—such as assessing the patient's individual constitution (Prakriti), understanding their digestive capacity (Agni), and modifying everyday food habits (Pathya-Apathya)—are tailored to the person.

Clinical Philosophy & Patient Care

Today, Dr. Apkesha practices both through modern teleconsultations for patients across India and worldwide, and through in-person consultations in Lunawada, Mahisagar, Gujarat. She remains fluent in Gujarati, Hindi, and English, ensuring that patients and their families feel heard, respected, and fully supported throughout their healing journey.

Medical Disclaimer & Compliance Notice

Emergency Warning: If you are experiencing an acute medical emergency (such as severe chest pain, sudden numbness, difficulty breathing, or severe trauma), please call 108 or proceed immediately to the nearest hospital emergency room.

1. Nature of Consultations: All consultations, dietary recommendations, and herbal suggestions provided by Dr. Apkesha Patel (BAMS) are based on classical Ayurvedic medicine principles (Ashtanga Hridaya, Charaka Samhita, and Sushruta Samhita) and are intended for chronic health management, nutritional balance, and holistic wellness.

2. No Guaranteed Cures: Ayurveda evaluates individual metabolic responses and doshic constitution. Clinical outcomes vary from person to person. No claims of instant, magical, or guaranteed cures are made.

3. Existing Medications: Patients are advised never to discontinue or alter prescribed allopathic treatments (such as insulin, blood pressure medications, or thyroid hormones) without the direct consent of their treating allopathic physician. Ayurvedic management can be integrated safely with conventional care.

4. Teleconsultation Scope: Remote video and audio consultations have inherent limitations compared to in-person physical examinations. In situations where physical diagnosis is essential, Dr. Apkesha may recommend an in-person clinical visit or conventional diagnostic imaging.

Patient Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

1. Information Collection: When you submit an appointment request, we collect your name, contact phone number, preferred consultation date, time, and optional brief health notes.

2. Purpose of Use: Your personal and clinical details are used exclusively to schedule appointments, conduct consultations, and provide follow-up dosage and dietary guidance.

3. Strict Confidentiality: Your health records, diagnostic discussions, and personal identity are protected by doctor-patient confidentiality. We never sell, rent, or trade your contact or medical information to any third-party marketing entities.

4. Secure Messaging: Appointment notifications and communication are handled through encrypted channels (WhatsApp / Telephone). Pre-filled WhatsApp URLs generated on this website omit sensitive diagnosis details to protect your privacy.
