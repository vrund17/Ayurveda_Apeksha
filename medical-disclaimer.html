Medical Disclaimer & Compliance Notice

Practice of Dr. Apkesha Patel (BAMS), Gujarat, India

EMERGENCY MEDICAL ADVISORY: DIAL 108

If you or someone in your care is experiencing an acute medical emergency—such as acute chest pain, sudden breathlessness, signs of stroke (facial drooping, slurred speech), sudden loss of consciousness, severe trauma, or acute surgical abdomen—do NOT wait for an Ayurvedic appointment. Immediately call 108 (National Ambulance Service) or proceed to the nearest emergency hospital ICU.

1. Complementary and Holistic Healthcare Scope

The clinical consultations, dietary guidelines, and herbal recommendations provided by Dr. Apkesha Patel (BAMS) are based on traditional Ayurvedic principles (Ashtanga Hridaya, Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita) and evidence-informed public health experience. They are designed for holistic wellness, root-cause disease management, and metabolic recovery.

2. No Guaranteed or Instant Cures

Ayurveda honors individual bio-individuality (Prakriti). Human physiology and response times to dietary therapy, herbal formulations, and lifestyle realignment vary significantly from patient to patient depending on age, chronicity of the ailment, and digestive strength (Agni). Dr. Apkesha Patel makes no claims of guaranteed, instant, or miraculous medical cures.

3. Existing Allopathic Medications

Patients must never stop, reduce, or alter life-sustaining allopathic prescriptions (such as insulin, cardiovascular medications, hypertensive drugs, or thyroid supplements) without the direct consent and supervision of their primary allopathic physician. Ayurvedic treatment can be safely co-managed with conventional care under proper clinical guidance.

4. Scope and Limitations of Teleconsultations

Remote video and audio consultations provide valuable guidance, dietary planning, and herbal therapy. However, remote consultation has inherent clinical limitations and cannot fully substitute for a physical in-person examination or emergency diagnostic imaging (such as CT scans, MRIs, or urgent blood tests). If physical examination is necessary, Dr. Apkesha will advise an in-person clinical visit in Lunawada or at a local medical facility.

5. Professional Qualifications & Registration

Dr. Apkesha Patel holds the recognised degree of Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery (BAMS) and has served as an AYUSH Medical Officer under the Government of Gujarat's Primary Health Centre system. Registration credentials from the Gujarat Board of Ayurvedic & Unani Systems of Medicine are available upon request for verification.
