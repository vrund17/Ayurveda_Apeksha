The In-Person Advantage: Nadi Pariksha

When you consult in-person at our Lunawada clinic, Dr. Apkesha performs classical Nadi Pariksha (Ayurvedic Pulse Diagnosis) at the radial artery. Through rhythmic pulse movements, she detects:

  • Subtle imbalances in Vata, Pitta, and Kapha before they manifest as chronic conditions.
  • Deep tissue (Dhatu) depletion and toxicity levels (Ama).
  • Direct physical assessment of joints, spine mobility, skin texture, and abdominal tone.
How to Prepare for In-Person Nadi Pariksha:

For the most accurate pulse reading, it is recommended to maintain a light stomach (allow at least 2.5 hours after a meal) and bring any recent lab reports or current medications with you.
