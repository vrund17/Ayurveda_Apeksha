Patient Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

Effective Date: 2026 • Practice of Dr. Apkesha Patel (BAMS), Gujarat, India

Commitment to Patient Trust

Dr. Apkesha Patel is committed to upholding the highest standards of professional medical confidentiality, patient privacy, and ethical healthcare communication as mandated by relevant AYUSH medical ethics codes and Indian data protection standards.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or schedule an appointment, we may collect the following information:

  • Patient Contact Information: Full name, WhatsApp phone number, and optional email address.
  • Appointment Scheduling Details: Preferred consultation mode (Online video vs. In-person at Lunawada), requested date, and time slot.
  • Voluntary Clinical Context: Primary health category and brief, optional notes regarding past diagnoses or symptoms you choose to submit.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your details are used strictly for clinical and operational purposes:

  • To verify appointment availability and confirm your scheduled consultation slot.
  • To conduct your online video/audio session or prepare for your in-person clinic visit in Lunawada, Gujarat.
  • To transmit digital prescriptions, dietary guidance charts, and follow-up dosage recommendations.

3. Strict Confidentiality & Zero Third-Party Sharing

Your health discussions, medical history, and personal contact details are protected under professional doctor-patient privilege. We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal or medical data to commercial advertisers, marketing agencies, or data brokers.

4. Secure Communication & Messaging Protocols

For patient convenience, appointment confirmations and communications are facilitated through encrypted channels (WhatsApp / Telephone). Notice that pre-filled WhatsApp URLs generated on this website omit sensitive diagnostic details to protect your privacy in chat histories.

5. Your Rights as a Patient

You have the right to request access to your consultation history, update your contact details, or request the deletion of non-essential records by contacting Dr. Apkesha’s clinic desk at apexapatel316@gmail.com.
